IVI GROUP OU — an international company in fishing, processing and seafood trade

IVI GROUP OU is a company operating in the fishing industry and the international trade of fish and seafood. Our main activities include fish harvesting, fish processing, wholesale trade, import and export.

Over the course of its operations, the company has established itself as a reliable supplier of fish products in European and international markets. Our products are exported to more than 12 countries. We also import products from our international partners, expanding cooperation opportunities for our customers.

The group includes fishing and fish-processing companies in Chile, China and Argentina.

We pay particular attention to product quality at every stage: from harvesting and processing to storage, transportation and delivery. Products are stored in modern warehouse facilities intended to maintain appropriate storage conditions and product quality.

Logistics is an important part of our operations. We work to organize efficient transportation and timely delivery in accordance with agreed supply terms and customer requirements.

IVI GROUP OU is a team of professionals with experience in the fishing industry and international trade. We aim to build long-term business relationships by offering a professional approach, quality products, competitive commercial terms and relevant market information.

We value the trust of our customers and partners and take responsibility for the quality of supplied products, compliance with agreements and fulfilment of our commitments.
